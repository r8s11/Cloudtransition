## Rules

1. **The job comes first.** Reputation sets the tone for extensions, conversion, and references.
2. **The work log is non-negotiable.** Three bullets a day. It becomes MD-102 prep, playbook content, automation candidates, and resume bullets.
3. **The bills lane outranks the journey lane** when time collides — playbook and MD-102 before DevOps extras. But the DevOps block doesn't die; it's one block, protected.
4. **Builds, not courses.** Every block produces an artifact (a module, a tool, a tutorial page). No passive video-watching blocks.
5. **Tutorial format everywhere.** Each build gets its HTML guide — that's the learning method AND the portfolio.
6. **Debt payments are part of the routine**, not a someday project. Weekly check, automated payments.
7. **Contract-end is the re-plan trigger.** Any duration signal → update dashboard, refresh resumes, re-open the pipeline *early*. The plan that worked: [plans/job-search-plan-2026-05.md](plans/job-search-plan-2026-05.md).
8. **Rest is productive.** Zero days are allowed; the desperate pace ended in June.
