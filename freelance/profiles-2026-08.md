## Rate sheet (set 2026-08-10, market-checked)

- **Upwork hourly:** start at **$45–50/hr** to win the first 2–3 reviews fast (new profile, zero rating = trust gap); raise to **$60–70/hr** once you have 3+ five-star jobs. Comparable US-market Intune/Entra/M365 admin work runs $45–70/hr for solid intermediate work, $75–125+/hr once specialized/reviewed. Upwork's freelancer service fee is roughly **10%** — you net ~90% of billed hours (verify current rate in-app; it has changed before). **Payout lag:** hourly earnings are billed weekly (Mon–Sun) and released ~10 days later — before any cash lands, first set up tax info + a withdrawal method. Budget the lag into the 2–4 week runway: hours worked in week 1 pay out around week 2–3, not immediately.
- **TaskRabbit:** TaskRabbit doesn't publish a category rate — before listing, open the Computer Help category for NYC/Brooklyn and price **at or slightly under the median of the top 5 Taskers with 50+ reviews** (undercutting a new, unreviewed profile is how you get picked). As a starting anchor: **$50–65/hr**, one-hour minimum. Taskers keep **100% of their set rate plus 100% of tips/reimbursements** — TaskRabbit's service/trust fee is charged to the *client* on top, not deducted from your payout. Confirm the current payout schedule in-app (typically a few business days after task completion, not instant).
- **Flat-rate local (Nextdoor/FB groups/word of mouth):** **$50–75 flat** per basic visit (setup, WiFi fix, printer, smart-home device) — no platform fee, cash/instant payment apps possible same-day, fastest to convert, best for building the first reviews/testimonials to paste into Upwork/TaskRabbit later.
- **Net vs. gross, and travel:** TaskRabbit and local flat-rate pay close to 100% of what's billed (minus your own travel time/transit cost — budget ~$5–15/gig in NYC). Upwork nets ~90%. This is 1099/self-employment income — nothing is withheld automatically; **set aside ~25–30% of whatever you net for taxes owed next filing season** rather than treating it as fully spendable now.

## $3,000-in-2–4-weeks sprint (locked 2026-08-10)

**Target clarification: $3,000 net cash-in-hand, not $3,000 gross billed.** After Upwork's ~10% fee, NYC transit/travel costs, and setting aside ~25–30% for taxes on top of that (see above), the realistic **gross billing target is closer to $3,600–4,200** depending on the lane mix, to actually clear $3,000 spendable. Lean on TaskRabbit/local flat-rate (near-100% net) over Upwork (90% net) where possible. At a $50–65 blended rate that's roughly **55–75 billable hours** over 2–4 weeks around a full-time job — aggressive; success depends on running all lanes in parallel and converting clients in week 1, since Upwork payouts alone lag ~10 days behind hours worked.

**If the pipeline is too slow by day 7–10:** re-check gig-app approval status and staffing-agency response; neither is instant, so the check-in is about whether they're in motion, not a switch you flip that day.
