## STAR Pulls — 3 Pre-Loaded Stories

The recruiter screen rarely goes deep on STAR, but if they ask "tell me about a time…" you want one of these ready.

### Story 1: Built something from scratch — **Jira Service Management at Ventus**

- **S:** Joined Ventus Therapeutics; IT support was happening through email and Slack DMs with no system of record, no SLAs, no request catalog.
- **T:** I owned standing up a real ticketing platform for 100+ users.
- **A:** I evaluated options, picked Jira Service Management, designed the request types and workflows, defined SLAs by ticket category, built out the request catalog, set up reporting, and trained the team on it.
- **R:** First IT ticketing system the company ever had. Gave us SLA visibility, reduced repeat questions through the knowledge base, and made onboarding/offboarding workflows enforceable instead of tribal.

### Story 2: Scaling Apple admin — **ABM + Intune ADE at Peak Scientific**

- **S:** Peak Scientific's macOS/iOS fleet was being provisioned manually — slow, inconsistent, error-prone, and didn't scale with new-hire volume across North America.
- **T:** Stand up automated, zero-touch Mac/iOS provisioning that worked across multiple sites.
- **A:** Set up Apple Business Manager, integrated it with Intune MDM, configured automated device enrollment, built out configuration profiles and app deployment policies, defined the compliance baseline and conditional access tied to device state.
- **R:** Zero-touch enrollment for the company's macOS and iOS fleet. New-hire Mac setup went from manual hours to drop-shipping the laptop directly to the user.

### Story 3: Cross-region collaboration — **Global IT handovers at Peak Scientific**

- **S:** Peak Scientific runs 24/7 support across regions; I was on the North America team coordinating with India and Europe.
- **T:** Maintain seamless coverage with no gaps as tickets crossed time-zone boundaries.
- **A:** I established clear handover notes per ticket, used Zendesk as the source of truth so anyone could pick up context, scheduled brief overlap syncs at handoff windows, and proactively flagged in-flight escalations before going off-shift.
- **R:** Maintained 95%+ SLA compliance, no resolution gaps reported during handovers, and the pattern got reused on chronic-issue investigations that needed multiple regions to chase.
