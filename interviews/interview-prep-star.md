# Interview Prep — STAR Stories

Use these frameworks to prepare for behavioral interviews. Each story follows the STAR method:
**Situation → Task → Action → Result**

Write 8-10 stories from your IT Support contractor experience. Good stories can be reused across multiple questions.

---

## Common Behavioral Questions & Story Mapping

### 1. Tell me about a time you solved a difficult technical problem.

- **Situation:** [Describe the context — system outage, complex ticket, recurring issue]
- **Task:** [What was your responsibility?]
- **Action:** [Step-by-step what you did — diagnosis, research, solution]
- **Result:** [Outcome — time saved, issue resolved, prevented recurrence. Quantify if possible]

### 2. Describe a time you worked under pressure or tight deadlines.

- **Situation:** [High-severity incident, multiple urgent tickets, deadline-driven project]
- **Task:** [What needed to happen and by when?]
- **Action:** [How did you prioritize and execute?]
- **Result:** [Outcome and what you learned]

### 3. Tell me about a time you improved a process or automated something.

- **Situation:** [Manual, repetitive, or error-prone process you identified]
- **Task:** [What improvement was needed?]
- **Action:** [What did you build, script, or change?]
- **Result:** [Time saved, errors reduced, team adoption. Quantify]

### 4. Describe a time you had to learn something new quickly.

- **Situation:** [New technology, platform, or tool introduced at work]
- **Task:** [What did you need to learn and how fast?]
- **Action:** [Your learning approach — docs, courses, hands-on experimentation]
- **Result:** [How quickly you became productive. Impact on team]

### 5. Tell me about a time you disagreed with a coworker or manager.

- **Situation:** [Technical disagreement, process conflict, priority difference]
- **Task:** [What was at stake?]
- **Action:** [How did you communicate your perspective? How was it resolved?]
- **Result:** [Outcome and relationship impact]

### 6. Describe a project you're most proud of.

- **Situation:** [Context of the project]
- **Task:** [Your specific role and responsibilities]
- **Action:** [Key decisions and contributions you made]
- **Result:** [Impact — uptime, cost savings, user satisfaction, team efficiency]

### 7. Tell me about a time you made a mistake at work.

- **Situation:** [What happened?]
- **Task:** [What was the impact?]
- **Action:** [How did you handle it — communication, fix, prevention?]
- **Result:** [What changed as a result? What did you learn?]

### 8. Describe a time you helped a colleague or mentored someone.

- **Situation:** [Colleague struggling with a task, new team member onboarding]
- **Task:** [What support was needed?]
- **Action:** [How did you help — documentation, pair work, training?]
- **Result:** [Their improvement, team benefit]

## Technical Interview Prep

### Fundamentals to Review

- [ ] Linux basics — file system, permissions, processes, systemd, cron
- [ ] Networking — TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP/HTTPS, firewalls, load balancers, subnets
- [ ] Cloud concepts — IaaS/PaaS/SaaS, regions, availability zones, IAM
- [ ] CI/CD — what it is, why it matters, basic pipeline stages
- [ ] Containers — what Docker does, images vs containers, basic commands
- [ ] Version control — Git branching, merge vs rebase, pull requests

### Scenario Questions to Practice

- [ ] "How would you troubleshoot a web application that's returning 500 errors?"
- [ ] "Walk me through setting up a CI/CD pipeline for a web app."
- [ ] "A server is running out of disk space. How do you diagnose and fix it?"
- [ ] "How would you design a deployment strategy for zero downtime?"
- [ ] "Explain the difference between horizontal and vertical scaling."
- [ ] "How would you set up monitoring and alerting for a production service?"

### Questions to Ask Interviewers

- What does a typical day look like for this role?
- How does the team handle on-call rotations?
- What's the tech stack and how do you make decisions about tooling?
- What does career growth look like on this team?
- What's the biggest challenge the team is facing right now?
- How do you handle incidents and post-mortems?
