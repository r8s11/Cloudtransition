**Purpose & context**

Roosevelt is building FORGE, a comprehensive 30-week self-development system focused on dating, attraction, relationships, grounded masculinity, and fatherhood readiness. The project is explicitly ethics-anchored and science-grounded — Roosevelt's stated preference is for approaches rooted in research and honest self-development rather than manipulation-oriented tactics. The curriculum draws from a 55-book library spanning attraction psychology, relationship science, masculinity, sexuality, communication, and fatherhood. Critical perspectives on masculinity run as a deliberate guardrail thread throughout, with controversial texts always paired with counterweight sources.

Key collaborator: ROOK, a deployable AI coaching persona designed to run on smaller Claude models (Haiku/Flash) as a daily accountability coach within the FORGE system.

Roosevelt is also navigating a relatively recent move to a new city, which informs goals around building social infrastructure and provides a natural conversational frame in dating contexts.

**Key learnings & principles**

- **Concrete ask over vague invitation**: Specific, actionable invites consistently outperform open-ended or standing offers — both in dating contexts and as a general principle Roosevelt already operates with
- **Science before tactics before critique**: The recommended reading sequence within FORGE prioritizes foundational research before applied strategy before critical deconstruction
- **Ethics as architecture, not afterthought**: Guardrails against manipulation, entitlement, and objectification are hard-coded into ROOK and woven through curriculum design — not treated as optional commentary
- **Counter-read protocol**: Provocative or controversial texts (e.g., The Rational Male) are never consumed in isolation; they are always paired with critical counterweights (e.g., bell hooks, Laura Bates)
- **Duplicate risk is real**: Multiple sessions have rebuilt overlapping FORGE artifacts; checking for existing files before generating new ones is an established protocol
- **Covert "testing" mindsets undermine authenticity**: In dating contexts, evaluative or strategic framing can erode the genuine quality of interactions
