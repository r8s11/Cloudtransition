## Key learnings & principles
- [stated] Data creates urgency better than confrontation: letting throughput math surface the problem (devices remaining vs. weeks left vs. required pace) works better than direct criticism of the IT manager
- [stated] Align before proposing: co-owning initiatives with the IT manager rather than going around them is politically safer and more likely to succeed
- [stated] Post-meeting written recaps serve as both factual records and gentle accountability tools
- [stated] Reframe counterparts as fellow victims of bureaucracy rather than its source when trying to restore momentum without damaging relationships
- [stated] ITAM pitch language: speak to management in cost, risk/security, and compliance terms; avoid leading with tool spend; use a phased crawl-walk-run ask
- [stated] Scope USMT as a gap-filler only — OneDrive KFM handles the majority of migration needs; USMT covers what KFM doesn't (PSTs, local-only folders, app data)
- [stated] ESP configuration and pre-provisioning are the highest-leverage variables for improving Autopilot deployment throughput

## Approach & patterns
- [stated] Prefers diplomatically effective strategies over direct confrontation; works through relationships and systems
- [stated] Values concise, direct, technically grounded communication with scannable structure
- [stated] Favors practical, portable reference formats (checklists, one-pagers, markdown files) over abstract advice
- [stated] Approaches sensitive workplace dynamics carefully — sequencing rapport before raising friction, verbal before written
- [stated] Uses structured discovery questions to build situational awareness early in an engagement rather than assuming environment details
- [stated] Tends toward organized, phased approaches (crawl-walk-run) when proposing new initiatives

## Tools & resources
- [stated] Deployment: Microsoft Autopilot, Intune, SCCM/MDT, Entra ID (including hybrid join and Temporary Access Pass), Enrollment Status Page (ESP), white glove/pre-provisioning mode
- [stated] Migration: OneDrive Known Folder Move (KFM), USMT (as gap-filler), Files On-Demand
- [stated] Imaging/provisioning: Windows ICD, provisioning packages (.ppkg)
- [stated] Hardware: Lenovo laptops (primary refresh), Dell laptops (triage/inventory/repurpose)
- [stated] Asset management: Dell TechDirect (bulk warranty lookup), ServiceNow (ITSM/ITAM), Excel (client tracker)
- [stated] Reference materials: discovery question framework, deployment-phase checklists (KFM, USMT, ICD, Autopilot), ITAM one-pager draft
