- [stated] Roosevelt is a contractor working at a client site on a large-scale laptop deployment and refresh project
- [stated] Primary goal: deploy Lenovo Windows 11 laptops to end users by end of summer
- [stated] Success is defined as apps installed and OneDrive restored per device
- [stated] Broader engagement goals: build credibility, establish good working relationships, and potentially formalize IT Asset Management (ITAM) practices at the client
- [stated] Key counterpart is a client IT manager; Roosevelt has worked to align with rather than bypass them, and has raised concern about their follow-through and pace of progress
- [stated] Project involves coordination with business owners and IT stakeholders across multiple domains
- [stated] Roosevelt is a newcomer to this client environment, which shapes a preference for relationship-building over direct confrontation and for politically careful moves (co-owning proposals, establishing rapport before raising friction)

## Current state
- [stated] Deployment is underway, ramping from a slow start toward a significantly higher weekly throughput needed to hit the end-of-summer target
- [stated] A stalled period with insufficient meetings and follow-through from the IT manager has been a friction point; Roosevelt is surfacing this diplomatically using data and structured questions rather than direct criticism
- [stated] Roosevelt lacked visibility into the client IT manager's Excel tracking sheet — this may be an ongoing gap
- [stated] Current practice of collecting user passwords during setup was flagged as uncomfortable; Temporary Access Pass (TAP) via Entra identified as the preferred alternative, to raise once rapport is established
- [stated] A one-pager was drafted proposing formal ITAM to management, framed around the refresh work already generating clean asset data, with the IT manager positioned as co-sponsor

## On the horizon
- [stated] Raise the TAP/password issue verbally once sufficient rapport with the IT manager exists
- [stated] Accelerate deployment throughput toward the pace required to hit the end-of-summer deadline
- [stated] Potentially formalize the ITAM proposal through the IT manager
- [stated] Complete laptop triage and inventory for older Dell devices being repurposed or retired
- [stated] Possible consolidation of discovery and checklist materials into portable reference formats
