Job Details
Description
Porchlight, from Danny Meyer’s Union Square Hospitality Group, is a true New York neighborhood bar. Since 2015, we've focused on serving up cold drinks with warm hospitality. American bar fare, classic cocktails, live music and good times in the heart of Chelsea. Come on in!



Who you are:

The Barback is an important partner to our Bar and Service teams and is responsible for ensuring that our Bar team has everything they need in order to create memorable moments of hospitality for our guests. The Barback is also responsible for keeping all components of the bar area clean, organized, and stocked.

What you’ll do:

Consistently support a culture of Enlightened Hospitality
Create a welcoming and hospitable environment for all guests at the bar
Stock the bar with necessary product and service tools
Prepare necessary garnishes, syrups, juices, and/or batches
Maintain cleanliness and organization of the bar area, including the seating area
Follows proper steps of service with grace, while maintaining a sense of urgency throughout service
Communicates effectively with staff members, management, and the kitchen during service
Perform additional responsibilities as needed and other duties as assigned to support team objectives and operational success
What we need from you:

2+ years experience as a Barback role in a full-service restaurant
English fluency and additional languages preferred, but not required
Ability to stand or walk for long periods of time
Ability to balance & carry multiple plates, glass racks and or/glasses, and lift objects up to 30 lbs.
