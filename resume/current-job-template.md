## Writing Guide

### Strong Action Verbs (from your existing bullets)
Deliver, Manage, Maintain, Troubleshoot, Coordinate, Administer, Implement, Deploy, Establish, Lead, Analyze, Own, Partner, Build, Design

### What to Bold
Bold 1-3 bullets that represent your **biggest impact** — things you built, led, or introduced. Examples from your resumes:
- "**Implemented Jira Service Management from scratch**"
- "**Led workstation migration for the OneView Windows 10 upgrade**"
- "**Deployed GitHub Enterprise** for the development team"

### Scope & Metrics to Include
- Number of users/employees supported
- Team size or geographic reach
- SLA compliance percentages
- Ticket volume or resolution rates
- Systems/tools you manage
- Projects delivered or processes improved

### Tailoring Tips
After writing the base entry, adjust emphasis per resume variant:
- **DevOps/Cloud** → Highlight automation, CI/CD, cloud tools, scripting
- **Systems/Infra** → Highlight AD, networking, server management, infrastructure projects
- **IT Engineer** → Highlight user support scope, ITSM tools, stakeholder management
