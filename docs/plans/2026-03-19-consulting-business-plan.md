# Consulting Business Plan: Hybrid Ladder Strategy

**Author:** Roosevelt Segura
**Date:** 2026-03-19
**Goal:** Build a consulting practice that bridges from current IT expertise to DevOps/Cloud engineering, generating $500–$1,500/month starting immediately and scaling as skills grow.

---

## Strategy: The Hybrid Ladder

Start with IT operations consulting (skills you have today) to generate income and client relationships. As Docker, CI/CD, Terraform, and Kubernetes skills mature over the next 6–12 months, layer in cloud/DevOps services — upselling existing clients or attracting new ones.

**Time commitment:** 10–15 hours/week (trading some learning time for paid client work, which doubles as learning)
**Target first client:** Within 6 weeks of launch

## 2. Client Acquisition Strategy

### Primary Channels (Month 1–3)

**Freelance Platforms**
- **Upwork** — profile focused on "IT Support Specialist | AWS & Azure Certified." Bid on M365 admin, IT troubleshooting, and cloud setup gigs.
- **Fiverr Pro** — packaged services at fixed prices ("M365 Tenant Setup — $500", "IT Security Audit — $750"). Fixed-price packages convert better than hourly for new consultants.

**LinkedIn Outreach (Ongoing)**
- Headline: *"IT Infrastructure Consultant | AWS & Azure Certified | Helping Small Teams Get Enterprise-Grade IT"*
- Post 1–2x/week: quick tips, lessons from IT career, "here's what I fixed this week" stories
- Bilingual posts occasionally — tap the NYC Spanish-speaking business community

**Local NYC Small Business**
- BNI or local networking groups — in-person groups where small business owners trade referrals
- NYC SCORE mentors — free small business mentoring org; mentors often refer mentees to IT consultants
- Target verticals: biotech startups (domain knowledge), small medical offices (HIPAA knowledge), restaurants/retail needing basic IT

### Secondary Channels (Month 3+)

**Subcontracting**
- Reach out to small MSPs (Managed Service Providers) in NYC who need overflow help
- They handle sales, you do the work — lower rate but zero client acquisition effort

**Referrals**
- After 2–3 completed engagements, ask for testimonials and referrals
- Offer a small discount on future work for referrals that convert

### Timeline to First Client

| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1–2 | Profiles live on Upwork + Fiverr, LinkedIn updated |
| 3–4 | Bidding on 3–5 gigs/week, first LinkedIn posts |
| 5–6 | First engagement closed |

## 3. Packaged Offerings & Pricing

Fixed-price packages close faster and feel less risky to small business buyers than open-ended hourly billing.

### Starter Packages

| Package | What's Included | Price |
|---------|----------------|-------|
| **M365 Health Check** | Audit tenant config, security defaults, licensing optimization, written report with recommendations | $500 |
| **New Employee IT Setup** | M365 account, device enrollment (Intune), email, Teams, security groups, documented runbook for future hires | $300/employee |
| **IT Security Baseline** | MFA enforcement, conditional access policies, endpoint protection review, SSO config, 1-page security posture report | $750 |
| **Help Desk in a Box** | Set up Jira or Zendesk from scratch, create ticket categories, SLA policies, 3 canned response templates, 1-hour training | $1,200 |
| **Monthly IT Retainer** | 10 hours/month of on-call IT support, priority response, monthly health report | $750/month |

### Why Packages Work

- **Easier to say yes to** — "$500 for an M365 audit" beats "I charge $75/hr, not sure how long it'll take"
- **Scope is clear** — protects from scope creep
- **Stackable** — a client who buys the Health Check often adds the Security Baseline
- **The retainer is the goal** — one $750/month retainer + one gig = $1,500 target hit

## 4. Business Basics & Legal

### Business Structure

- **Start as sole proprietor** — no paperwork needed in NY, just report income on Schedule C
- **Register an LLC later** ($200 NY filing fee) once consistently earning $1,000+/month
- **Get an EIN from the IRS** (free, 5 minutes online) — use instead of SSN on contracts

### Essential Tools

| Need | Tool | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| Invoicing | Wave or Invoice Ninja | Free |
| Contracts | Bonsai or simple template | Free–$19/mo |
| Time tracking | Toggl | Free |
| Business email | Google Workspace or Zoho Mail | Free–$6/mo |
| Portfolio/landing page | GitHub Pages or Carrd | Free–$19/yr |

### Protect Yourself

- **Always use a contract** — scope of work, payment terms (50% upfront for projects), exclusions, liability limits
- **Set payment terms** — Net 15 for projects, prepaid for retainers. Never start without a deposit on fixed-price packages
- **Track expenses** — domain, tools, home office percentage, internet — all deductible
- **Separate bank account** — not required as sole proprietor, but makes taxes dramatically easier

### Don't Overthink

No logo, business name, LLC, or website needed for first client. Clean LinkedIn + Upwork account + simple contract template is enough to start.

## 6. Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| **No clients in first 6 weeks** | Medium | Low | Lower prices for first 2 gigs to build reviews. Subcontract for an MSP to guarantee some work. |
| **Scope creep on projects** | High | Medium | Always use contracts with clear scope. "That's a great idea — let me quote that as a separate engagement." |
| **Client doesn't pay** | Low | High | 50% deposit upfront, always. Net 15 terms. Stop work immediately if payment is late. |
| **Burnout from job + consulting + learning** | Medium | High | Hard cap at 15 hrs/week consulting. Drop learning hours before sleep. Protect weekends. |
| **Underpricing your work** | High | Medium | Start at $60–$75/hr minimum, never lower. Packages help avoid hourly rate pressure. |
| **Client expects 24/7 availability** | Medium | Medium | Set expectations in contract: business hours response, emergency surcharge for after-hours. |
| **Imposter syndrome on Tier 2 services** | High | Medium | Only offer Tier 2 after you've built it in a project. Your portfolio IS your credential. |

### Burnout Prevention (Priority Risk)

Consulting replaces some learning time (client work IS learning), not stacks on top. If stretched:

1. Drop Upwork bidding first (passive income sources like retainers stay)
2. Reduce learning to maintenance mode (review, don't build new)
3. Never sacrifice sleep or primary job performance
