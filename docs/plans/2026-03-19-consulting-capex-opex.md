# Consulting Practice: CapEx & OpEx Analysis

**Companion to:** [Consulting Business Plan](2026-03-19-consulting-business-plan.md)

This document breaks down capital and operating expenses for launching and running the consulting practice, with break-even analysis.

---

## 1. Capital Expenditures (CapEx)

One-time investments to get started. The goal is to keep this under $500 — you likely already own most of what you need.

### Required (If You Don't Already Have)

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Laptop | $0–$1,200 | You likely already have one. If buying, a refurbished ThinkPad or MacBook is fine |
| External monitor | $0–$200 | Nice to have for multi-tasking during client calls. Skip if budget-tight |
| Headset with mic | $30–$80 | Essential for client calls. A basic USB headset works |
| Webcam | $0–$60 | Most laptops have one. Upgrade if video quality matters for sales calls |

### Optional (Invest Later)

| Item | Cost | When |
|------|------|------|
| Standing desk or desk upgrade | $100–$300 | After first $1,000 earned |
| Second monitor | $150–$250 | After retainer client secured |
| Business cards | $20–$50 | Only if doing BNI/local networking |
| UPS / battery backup | $80–$150 | When doing remote admin work on client systems |

### Realistic CapEx Budget

| Scenario | Total |
|----------|-------|
| **Already equipped** (laptop, headset, decent setup) | $0–$50 |
| **Need basics** (headset, webcam) | $50–$150 |
| **Starting from scratch** (laptop + peripherals) | $500–$1,500 |

**Your situation:** You already have a working dev setup (running React/FastAPI projects, multiple tools). Realistic CapEx is likely **$0–$100** (maybe a better headset).

## 3. Tax Obligations (Hidden OpEx)

These aren't monthly bills, but they're real costs that new consultants often forget.

| Tax | Rate | Frequency | Notes |
|-----|------|-----------|-------|
| Federal income tax | 12–22% bracket (on net profit) | Quarterly estimated | Form 1040-ES |
| Self-employment tax | 15.3% (Social Security + Medicare) | Quarterly estimated | On top of income tax |
| NY State income tax | 4–6.85% | Annual (with quarterly estimated) | Depends on total income |
| NYC income tax | 3.078–3.876% | Annual | City residents only |

### The 30% Rule

**Set aside 30% of every consulting payment** in a dedicated savings account for taxes. This covers federal + state + SE tax for most scenarios in your income range.

| Monthly Consulting Income | 30% Tax Reserve | Take-Home After Taxes |
|--------------------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| $500 | $150 | $350 |
| $1,000 | $300 | $700 |
| $1,500 | $450 | $1,050 |

### Tax Deductions That Reduce Your Bill

Everything in the OpEx tables above is deductible, plus:
- Home office: $5/sq ft, up to 300 sq ft = up to $1,500/year deduction
- Internet: % used for business (typically 25–50%)
- Phone: % used for business
- Professional development: certs, courses, books, conferences
- Mileage: $0.67/mile for client site visits (2025 rate, check IRS for 2026)

## 4. Break-Even Analysis

### Month 1–3 (Tier 1 OpEx: ~$0/month)

| Revenue Needed | To Cover |
|----------------|----------|
| $0/month | Operating costs (essentially free) |
| 1 gig at $500 | Proves the model works |
| $500–$750/month | Low end of income goal after 30% tax reserve |

**Break-even: Immediate.** With near-zero costs, your first dollar of revenue is profit (minus tax reserve).

### Month 3–6 (Tier 2 OpEx: ~$75/month)

| Revenue Needed | To Cover |
|----------------|----------|
| $75/month | Operating costs |
| $107/month | OpEx + 30% tax reserve on the $107 |
| $750–$1,500/month | Income goal range |

**Break-even: ~1.5 billable hours/month at $75/hr.** Very achievable.

### Month 6+ (Tier 3 OpEx: ~$150/month)

| Revenue Needed | To Cover |
|----------------|----------|
| $150/month | Operating costs |
| $215/month | OpEx + 30% tax reserve |
| $1,500–$3,000/month | Scaling income goal |

**Break-even: ~2 billable hours/month at $75/hr.** Still very low.

## 6. Key Takeaways

1. **Startup cost is near zero.** You already have the equipment and can use free tools. No reason to delay.
2. **Break-even is immediate.** With $0–$75/month in OpEx, every gig is profitable from day one.
3. **The 30% tax rule is non-negotiable.** Self-employment tax surprises kill freelance businesses. Reserve it from the first dollar.
4. **Scale OpEx with revenue, not before.** Don't pay for LinkedIn Premium or Bonsai until you're earning. Free tools first.
5. **One retainer client changes everything.** A single $750/month retainer covers all operating costs and provides a predictable income floor.
6. **Conservative scenario still hits your $500–$1,500 goal by month 4–6.** Even slow-starting, this math works.
