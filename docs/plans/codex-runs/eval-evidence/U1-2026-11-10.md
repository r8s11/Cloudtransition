## Defect found and fixed during this re-verification

Before the fix, 12 of the 22 OpenCode entries and both OpenRouter entries offered default, low, medium, high. Their ids contain a colon (`…:free`), and `static/app.js` split `provider:id` on every colon, so the catalogue lookup missed and the picker fell back to the generic effort list. The server rejects those efforts with 400. `splitModel()` now splits at the first colon only. The counts above are from after the fix, with the page reloaded.
