## Why AS → BS in CS

1. **AS is a milestone** — standalone credential on your resume ~1 year before BS. Risk mitigation if life interrupts.
2. **Strongest engineering foundation** — algorithms, data structures, full-stack dev, software security. These fundamentals apply to DevOps, SRE, platform engineering, and software engineering roles.
3. **Resume signal** — "Computer Science" clears more HR filters for engineering-track roles than "Information Technology." Your certs and portfolio take over in the interview.
4. **Cloud/DevOps gap is filled by certs** — AWS SA + AZ-204 + AZ-400 + hands-on Terraform/K8s projects give more cloud credibility than any single IT course.
5. **Math is front-loaded** — Precalculus done. Calculus I via Sophia ($99 vs $342). Discrete Math and Physics are the real hurdles but they're one term.

### Why IT Stays on the Table (After CS)

A second bachelor's in IT (BA or BS) after CS would be fast and cheap:

- Gen Ed (42 cr) — **already done** from CS degree
- Shared courses (IT-140 Python, IT-145 Java, DAD-220 Databases) — **already done**
- Remaining IT-specific courses: ~30-40 credits
- IT adds cloud computing (IT-423), networking, and project management — skills that complement CS
- Only pursue if career direction shifts or employer requires it; certs likely cover the same ground
