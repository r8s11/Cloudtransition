## Why This Pathway

### Saylor → Sophia → SNHU (Cost Priority)

- **Saylor**: Free. Up to 15 credits for $0. No reason not to do first.
- **Sophia**: $99/mo unlimited. IT 140 + ENG 190 alone save ~$2,000 vs SNHU.
- **Study.com**: $95 for 2 targeted courses not on Sophia.
- **SNHU**: Reserved for courses only available at SNHU (upper-level CS, discrete math, physics, capstone).

### AS First (Phase 4)

- **Standalone credential** — goes on resume ~1 year before BS
- **Risk mitigation** — if life interrupts, you have a degree
- **Zero waste** — every AS credit counts toward BS

### BS in CS (Phase 5)

- **Strongest engineering foundation** — algorithms, data structures, full-stack dev
- **Resume signal** — "Computer Science" clears more HR filters than "IT"
- **Cloud/DevOps gap filled by certs** — AWS SA + AZ-204 + AZ-400 + projects
