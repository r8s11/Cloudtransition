> **Picking guidance.** Pick a topic that meets all four:
> 1. **Genuine multiple viewpoints** — the syllabus explicitly requires "a topic with multiple viewpoints." Avoid one-sided issues.
> 2. **You actually care.** 8 weeks of reading on a topic you don't care about will be miserable.
> 3. **Has scholarly sources** — search SNHU's Shapiro Library before committing. If only blog posts come up, pick something else.
> 4. **Narrow enough to argue in one essay.** "Cloud computing" is too broad; "Should the federal government mandate FedRAMP equivalents for state-level cloud contracts?" is the right scale.
>
> Tech-adjacent topics that work for someone in your career path: data privacy regulation, AI in hiring, open-source vs. proprietary in critical infrastructure, right-to-repair, vendor lock-in in cloud, accessibility law for digital products, ethics of biometric authentication.
