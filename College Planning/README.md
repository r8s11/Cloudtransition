Gen Ed and science courses only — no CS or IT courses. Sophia covers the same courses cheaper (unlimited at $99/mo). Skip unless you need a specific science lab course.

### Credit for Prior Learning (CPL)

SNHU accepts **up to 90 transfer credits** toward a bachelor's (75% of the program).

Accepted sources include:

- **Industry Certifications**: AWS, CompTIA (A+, Network+, Security+), Google, IBM, Microsoft
- **Course Providers**: Sophia Learning, Study.com, StraighterLine
- **Exams**: AP, CLEP
- **Military**: Training and experience credits accepted

Note: edX and Coursera are **not** direct SNHU transfer partners. However, specific professional certificates hosted on those platforms (Google, IBM) are accepted via the CPL catalog.
