# Networking Outreach Templates

Use these as starting points — personalize each message with something specific to the person.

---

## 1. Former Colleague (Warm Reconnect)

> **Subject:** Quick career catch-up?
>
> Hey [Name],
>
> Hope you're doing well! I've been thinking about people I enjoyed working with at [Company], and you came to mind.
>
> I'm making a move into DevOps / Cloud Engineering — building on my IT Support background and AWS + Azure certifications. I'd love to hear what you're up to these days and catch up for 15 minutes if you're open to it.
>
> No pressure at all — just reconnecting. Let me know if you have time this week or next.
>
> Best,
> Roosevelt

---

## 2. Former Colleague (Direct Referral Ask)

> **Subject:** Career transition — would love your input
>

>

>
> Totally understand if the timing doesn't work. Appreciate you either way.
>
> Roosevelt

## 6. Follow-Up After Coffee Chat / Call

> Hey [Name],
>
> Thanks so much for taking the time to chat today — I really appreciated hearing about [specific thing they mentioned]. Your point about [insight] really stuck with me.
>
> I'll definitely [action item you discussed — e.g., "check out that Terraform course" / "look into roles at Company X"].
>
> Let's stay in touch. Happy to return the favor anytime.
>
> Best,
> Roosevelt

---

## Tips

- **Personalize every message** — mention something specific (shared project, their recent post, their company)
- **Keep it short** — 3-5 sentences for LinkedIn, 1 short paragraph for email
- **Don't lead with the ask** — reconnect first, ask second
- **Follow up once** if no response after 5-7 days, then move on
- **Track every outreach** in networking/contacts.yml
