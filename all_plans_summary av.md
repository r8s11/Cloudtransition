## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Don't try to read everything first**
   - Start with Week 1 guide
   - Reference other plans as needed

2. **Update weekly goals every Sunday**
   - 30-minute review
   - Check off completed items
   - Plan next week

3. **Keep progress simple**
   - GitHub commits show daily work
   - Weekly reviews show progress
   - Deployed projects prove skills

4. **Use plans as references**
   - Don't memorize everything
   - Look up as you need
   - Focus on building

5. **Adjust as you go**
   - Plans are guidelines, not rules
   - Speed up or slow down as needed
   - Focus on understanding, not rushing
